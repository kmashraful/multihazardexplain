# Multi-Hazard-Resilience: A simulation-based Game

A serious game simulating multi-hazard adaptation planning under budget constraints.
Teams collaboratively select mitigation options (Levees, EWS, Land Use Planning) against five hazards (Tsunami, Flood, Fire, Earthquake, Hurricane).
The goal is to maximize community resilience, promoting systems thinking and collaborative decision-making.
Learning outcomes align with the Bathke et al. (2019) paper on using games for complex water management.

Bathke, D.J., Haigh, T., Bernadt, T., Wall, N., Hill, H. and Carson, A. (2019), Using Serious Games to Facilitate Collaborative Water Management Planning Under Climate Extremes. Journal of Contemporary Water Research & Education, 167: 50-67. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1936-704X.2019.03311.x
